01 / THE STORY

See self-evolution unfold.

LEARNING FROM VERIFIED IMAGINATION

Imagine the task.
Verify the experience.
Improve the policy.

World action models predict videos and actions together. But an imagined success can still lead to a failed execution.

EVO-WAM turns reliable, self-generated trajectories into training experience. It checks both task completion and video–action consistency, then learns from the verified prefixes.

No additional expert demonstrations.
No external execution of candidate actions.

How self-evolution works

03 / IMPROVE

Learn from experience.
Generate again.

Verified prefixes accumulate across tasks and rounds. Mix them with the original training data, fine-tune a shared world action model, and use the updated model to generate the next round of candidates.
